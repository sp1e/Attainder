# Surface brief: the play screen (docs/design/ui-draft)

Scope: the whole game interface in one view. Mode: Operate. Desktop only, first for the owner at 2560×1440.

Audience and job: one experienced player in long sessions. Each year he answers what the court puts to him, spends up to four orders, and turns the year.

Constraints: every function of today's seven tabs stays; documented and generated people stay labelled; the map is the game's own engine with real geometry, never a drawn stand-in.

Unresolved: what the year turn shows once the model has narrated; how the thread folds when a court has forty people; the paper-map look at the farthest zoom.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The court is drawn as a lineage on the land it rules. A thread of portrait medallions in the order of succession lies over a living map, and every matter starts in a person. It refuses the panel that covers the map and the seven-tab reading task.

OWN-WORLD: Oak-gall ink panels over shaded satellite terrain; vellum text; gold leaf for the crown, the heir's line and the one primary action; minium for what is urgent; verdigris for what is well. One period accent per start (Carolingian purple vellum) on panel heads and selection. EB Garamond for text and names, Cinzel capitals for small labels and realm names on the land. Round medallions, square panels, 1px rules.

STORY: He sees his realm, sees who needs him, answers, and turns the year. He believes the numbers because each opens its reasons.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The map fills the screen, tilted, with relief. Top: a 3.4rem bar with the realm, the date, six figures and the gold button Nästa år at the right. Under it the thread, ruler first and largest. Left, 23rem: Kräver dig with the most urgent matter open and its choices, then Handla själv. Right, 20.5rem: the selected person or realm. Bottom, 4.1rem: the six areas and the months ahead with their deadlines.

FORM: Stamtavlan, a genealogical roll, candidate 4 of 7 on the ordered list. seed:9eceb2688d860396

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
