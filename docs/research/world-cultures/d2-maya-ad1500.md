# Maya political-form research: 31 December 1500

This dossier covers Maní/Xiu, Sotuta/Cocom, Ah Kin Chel, Cehpech/Motul and Acalan/Itzamkanac. The [companion research data](d2-maya-ad1500.json) preserves 62 scoped claims, 21 source records and their individual review bindings. All five cases remain blocked for playable court implementation, with empty active-person arrays and no accepted territory.

The bookmark is an administrative research date. A sufficiently supported reign interval containing it can establish an officeholder under the research brief. The present gaps concern the particular source chains and contextual reconstructions in this dossier; they impose no universal requirement for an act on the exact day.
