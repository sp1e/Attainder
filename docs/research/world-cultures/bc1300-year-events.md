# BC 1300–1201 research candidate

This candidate adds eight historical event records and eight cited sources, with 40 English event-field translations and 16 Swedish source-display translations. The records concern Yin, Tollense, Lahuradeva, Talepakemalai, Kadesh, the Lion Gate at Mycenae, Merey's attack on Egypt and the Merneptah stele.

The original independent source review returned seven PASS and three HOLD verdicts. This candidate retains the seven PASS events and narrows Kadesh's location to the name explicitly present in the reviewed passage. It does not claim that the original review returned eight PASS verdicts. The Egyptian-Hittite treaty and the final Mycenae wall-building phase remain withheld in full.

Seven retained leaf values change: Kadesh's geography becomes “Kadesh”; Merey's geography becomes “Egypten”; the Lahuradeva locator changes from section A to section B; and four Mycenae source fields are limited to the verified Lion Gate passage and its approximate 1250 BC date. Every retained event date is unchanged. The full disputed or calibrated intervals remain intact, including the Talepakemalai phase extending to 1100 BC. Such endpoints establish neither annual occurrence nor researched coverage of later years.

The original treaty finding remains unresolved because the cited Met route was already blocked and was not retried or bypassed. The Mycenae document gives conflicting dates for the later fortification itself; separating cistern construction from fortification does not resolve that conflict. Kopanias was checked through the public abstract only. Earlier author retrieval statements remain unverified by the independent reviewer.

Searches addressed all nine working regions. No newly retained event represents the Americas, sub-Saharan Africa, Southeast Asia, the steppe or Central Asia. The represented regions also remain incomplete. Only 1300–1201 BC changes to partial coverage; empty years remain research gaps.

The prospective integration uses a separately final-reviewed baseline of 670 events and 607 sources. It would produce 678 events and 615 sources, with 3,130 partially researched years and 1,700 unresearched years. The other 4,730 coverage years retain exactly their previous statuses and notes; no year zero is introduced.

Publication remains gated on an independent final review of this exact reconciliation, all 56 bilingual pairs and the prospective integration, followed by verification that the actually merged catalog and coverage match the baseline snapshots. Proposed approval metadata is confined to candidate files until those gates pass. No runtime code, deployment or configuration change is part of this research batch.
