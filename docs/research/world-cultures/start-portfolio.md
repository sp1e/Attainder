Further work must establish political scope separately from culture, the exact bookmark date, a ruler alive and in authority on that date, succession, marriage and kin at the top, the court and seat, Getty TGN location, geometry and engine limits. This bounded pass supplies no representative historical case for the Americas, sub-Saharan Africa or Oceania. It also leaves most entities on the selected maps unresearched. Those gaps are research tasks, and the ranking offers no guarantee that the dates will produce complete or balanced starts.
