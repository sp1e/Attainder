## Evidence and chronology

Each event cites one Key Events entry of the Metropolitan Museum of Art's Heilbrunn Timeline of Art History, from the pages "Japan, 500–1000 A.D.", "China, 500–1000 A.D.", "Great Britain and Ireland, 500–1000 A.D.", "Egypt, 500–1000 A.D.", "Iran, 500–1000 A.D.", "Mexico, 500–1000 A.D.", "Korea, 500–1000 A.D.", "Western North Africa (The Maghrib), 500–1000 A.D.". Each source record names the entry by its date phrase and opening words. The research session read the page text only; no images, object records or primary editions were examined. The Met dates most entries with "ca.", and the records keep that as approximate dates or ranges, with `noteEn` where the entry leaves the year of a single act open. Central Teotihuacan is burned: `historicalStatus` is `uncertain` because the entry gives the date as tradition or the cause as a possibility.

## Review and withholding

A fresh Claude subagent received only the delivered files, opened every cited page and checked each field against its entry. It corrected three records: the Hōryūji note called the temple royal, the Xuanzang geography named Central Asia in place of Southeast Asia, and the Goguryeo note drew on the separate 676 entry. All corrections are applied. Every page loaded; the site redirects each URL to the same page under `/pt/toah/ht/`.

The fall of Baekje (660), Khosrow II's seizure of Egypt (616–628), Prince Shōtoku's mission to China (600), the arrival of Islam in China (about 651), the Longmen Vairocana (about 672–675), Wari and Tiwanaku building (about 600) and the westward Slav migration (about 600) were left for a later pass. Duplicate checks ran against the runtime catalog and every English dataset in this folder, by id and by historical episode.

Ruling: one institutional timeline supports each record, and each summary stays within its entry. This supplement does not establish complete regional or annual coverage. Compatible English runtime support remains pending; the gameplay catalog, legacy locales and coverage counts remain unchanged.
