# Assyrian succession oaths: second research pass

This separate English research dataset adds one political episode from 672 BCE, supported by one scholarly article. Its date concerns the oaths rather than a later accession. Interpretations of concealed motives and proof of compliance are excluded.

The comparison uses a fixed catalog of 873 runtime events and 33 English datasets containing 52 events, together with four private candidate batches. The existing Jerwan dataset remains unchanged. This is a bounded addition, not complete annual or regional coverage.

The dataset remains `awaiting-runtime-support`; publication does not add a playable event. Independent source and final data reviews passed, both with successful process exits. All six English texts and the full publication note were approved without historical corrections.

Dataset: [bc0700-second-pass-supplement.json](bc0700-second-pass-supplement.json). Root verified sixty-three frozen inputs, six text hashes, thirty-eight committed comparison files and seven private originals. The later BC0100 glassblowing, wreck and accession records are separate episodes.
