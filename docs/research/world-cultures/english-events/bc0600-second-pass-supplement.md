## Evidence and chronology

Each event cites one Key Events entry of the Metropolitan Museum of Art's Heilbrunn Timeline of Art History, from the pages "Eastern Europe and Scandinavia, 1000 B.C.–1 A.D." and "Oceania, 1000 B.C.–1 A.D.". The Manga'asi entry sits on the Oceania page (region code `oc`), whose timeline bar lists the period under Melanesia. Each source record names the entry by its date phrase and opening words. The research session read the page text only; no images, object records or primary editions were examined.

On 12 November 2026 the live Met pages returned HTTP 429 from a Vercel security checkpoint. The session made no attempt to get past it and read Internet Archive captures of the same URLs instead: Eastern Europe and Scandinavia dated 2026-02-14 and Oceania dated 2026-02-10. Each locator names its capture date and archive URL. The archive refused a first rapid batch of requests; after a pause the session fetched each capture once.

Both entries give spans with "ca.", and the records keep them as ranges with full endpoints. The Manga'asi range runs to AD 1200, beyond this window, as the format requires. Each note states that the range marks a tradition or art period and dates no single act.

## Review and withholding

No candidate was withheld. The catalog's Vanuatu record, `ev-bc1000-teouma-begravningsbruk`, concerns multi-stage burial at Teouma around 1000 BC: another site, another practice and a single approximate year outside the Manga'asi range. No catalog or English record mentions face urns or a Baltic cremation custom. The checks ran against the runtime catalog of 986 events and every English dataset in this folder, by id and by historical episode. The review fields anticipate the independent Claude check that follows this delivery.

An independent Claude check on 2026-11-12 reopened each archive capture and confirmed both records without corrections.
