# 2100–2001 BC: English research supplement

The [dataset](bc2100-supplement.json) contains one independently approved event, two source records and five English display fields. It concerns the adaptation of Indus sealing traditions in Persian Gulf trade networks. Compatible runtime support remains pending.

## Evidence and chronology

[Laursen's study](https://www.harappa.com/sites/default/files/pdf/The_westward_transmission_of_Indus_Valle.pdf), especially pages 129–131, places Gulf-type seal development within about 2100–2000 BC and wider Dilmun adoption around 2050 BC. The record retains that complete interval. It presents proposed merchant identities and movements as the author's reconstruction, without an exact invention or migration year.

[Parpola's encyclopedia proof](https://tuhat.helsinki.fi/ws/portalfiles/portal/127256327/Parpola_A_2012._Indus_Civilization_1750_BCE_._Proofs_of_pp._3_18_in_Brill_s_Encyclopedia_of_Hinduism_vol._4.pdf), proof page 4, gives the seal group's 2100–2000 BC range. This synthesis cites Laursen; it does not provide an independent excavation or chronological determination. The consulted file is a proof, and both PDFs were checked through extracted text.

The event concerns a regional administrative technology. Earlier catalog records about settlements on Bahrain and Failaka describe different developments. Deduplication also checked the BC2200 and AD300 English datasets; no source IDs, event IDs or historical episodes overlap.

## Review and withholding

The initial independent source and English review approved the Gulf-sealing event but held a separate law-collection candidate after it could not verify all required passages. The complete law event and its three unused sources remain outside this delivery. The original HOLD report remains unchanged.

A fresh final reviewer verified that the retained event and both sources match the author's original data, checked all five English fields and the original selection note, and confirmed complete source closure. Both review processes exited successfully. The final PASS applies to this one-event subset only.

Ruling: publish the unchanged approved subset and preserve the unresolved law candidate separately. Missing verification does not establish that the held claim is historically false, and approval of the other event does not resolve it.

This focused supplement does not establish complete annual or regional coverage. Qijia and Seima-related leads did not yield sufficiently bounded chronology.
