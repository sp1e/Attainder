# Palace construction and monumental burials around 1900 BCE

The [dataset](bc1900-supplement.json) contains two independently approved events, three sources and ten English display fields. Both events have importance 1.

This supplemental English research contains two events, supported by three references: the construction of the palace at Phaistos and monumental burial construction at Kerma. Both dates remain approximate. These are separate research records awaiting compatible runtime support; they do not change the game's existing catalog, language files or coverage totals.

The official Hellenic Heritage account explicitly dates the Phaistos palace to around 1900 BCE. The entry distinguishes that construction from the settlement's earlier occupation and later rebuilding. It does not adopt legendary founders or assign all surviving structures to the first palace.

The Kerma entry follows Matthieu Honegger's 2014 discussion of monumental graves, especially around 1900 BCE. It does not date the first elite burials, the adoption of wooden burial beds or the establishment of kingship to that year. A 2018 caption supplies context for an individual grave at roughly the same date. Both publications are by the same excavator; the caption is not independent confirmation of the whole development. Numerical grave dimensions are omitted because the passages distinguish pits, mounds and phase-level size comparisons.

The French PDF text and captions were read. Page-image verification was unavailable. Independent source review found an internal discrepancy in the 2014 book: the passage extracts as 2015–1750 BCE, whereas its cemetery phase diagram gives 2050–1750 BCE. Both readings remain visible in the corrected source metadata. Neither supplies a numeric endpoint for this event, which uses the separate, explicit approximation around 1900 BCE.

The candidate was checked against the existing 873-event runtime catalog, twenty English research datasets available during preparation, and the separately reviewed BC2000 wooden-bed candidate. The Phaistos episode is distinct from the existing Malia palace record. The Kerma episode is distinct from earlier occupation, the city foundation, the early burial and the wooden-bed transition. Publication still requires a check against any further intervening datasets.

A separate wheat-chronology lead was withheld after the publisher redirected the request to inaccessible authentication. No alternate copy was sought. Earlier held chronology questions and denied sources were not reopened. Searches across the wider regional inventory did not yield additional accepted episodes for this supplement; this is not a claim that the century or its regional gaps are complete.

Independent source review passed both event claims and requested one source-metadata correction for the internal phase-date discrepancy. The corrected candidate preserves every event field and numeric date. A fresh independent final review approved that candidate, all ten English display fields, both selection notes and this note. It verified forty-five frozen inputs and twelve text hashes; both review processes exited successfully. Sources are referenced only; no source prose, images or grave plans are redistributed, and existing attribution and AGPL notices remain unchanged.
