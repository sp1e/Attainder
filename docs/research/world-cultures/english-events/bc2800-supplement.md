It describes the combination of these elements with local traditions in the Three Lakes region as the Auvernier-Cordé style. The event has importance 1: a notable local cultural adaptation. Its date is approximate and establishes neither the settlement's foundation nor the first Corded Ware pottery or a particular migration.
