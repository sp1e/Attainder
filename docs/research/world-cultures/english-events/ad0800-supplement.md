# AD 800–899: architectural and hydraulic research supplement

The [dataset](ad0800-supplement.json) contains two independently approved events, four sources and ten English display fields. Both events have importance 1.

Two English research records cover the rebuilding of Foguang Temple's East Hall in 857 and construction of the Rawda Island nilometer in 861. The first concerns a hall within an older Buddhist temple. The second concerns a structure used to measure Nile levels for agricultural administration; it makes no claim that nilometers or a new tax system were invented that year.

The Egyptian ministry gives 247 AH/861, while Archnet gives 246–247 AH/861. Both supplied the modern year; no calendar conversion was calculated. The Foguang date is supported by the ICOMOS evaluation and a scholarly conservation abstract. No month or day is asserted for either episode. The evaluation was read as extracted PDF text. The source review obtained no image accessible for visual assessment. The conservation article was used at abstract level; its full text and linked materials were not inspected. Original inscriptions and physical structures were not inspected.

These records supplement existing coverage without claiming that every event or region in the century has been researched.

Independent source and English review approved the candidate unchanged. The original final review approved both events and all twelve text fields but held two public-note scope claims and the manifest's reading boundary. A separate correction review approved the narrower note and verified every input and text hash. All three processes exited successfully; the original HOLD remains preserved.
