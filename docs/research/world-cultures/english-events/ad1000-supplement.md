# AD 1000–1099: waterworks and merchant organization

The [dataset](ad1000-supplement.json) contains two independently approved events, two sources and ten English display fields. It concerns Kamalagyan's waterworks provisions in 1037 and a Tamil merchant-guild meeting at Barus in 1088, both in Indonesia and rated notable (importance 1). The years date the inscriptions and their recorded acts; they do not establish construction starts, port foundations or first arrivals.

The Kamalagyan source is a university-hosted historical study with transcription and translation. Barus is supported through Axelle Rougeulle's scholarly review of the published inscription edition. The underlying edition was not read, and no claim depends on doing so.

A fresh independent source and English review passed both events, both sources and all ten display fields. Integration then identified an unsupported category value in both records. A separate candidate changes only the two categories: Kamalagyan uses politics for its royal revenue and maintenance provision; Barus uses culture for its merchant organization's recorded meeting. These changes preserve every source, date, display field and selection note. The original source report remains preserved; its category judgments did not establish compliance with the project taxonomy.

A fresh final review confirmed exactly those two category changes, all ten unchanged display fields, both selection notes and deduplication against the runtime catalog and seven other English datasets.
