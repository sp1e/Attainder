# Early Beaker burials in Britain

The [dataset](bc2500-supplement.json) contains one independently approved event, two sources and five English display fields. The event has importance 2.

This English research supplement records the earliest British Beaker burials within their reported date range of 2450–2325 BCE. It describes a change in funerary practices, while preserving evidence that older customs continued. The date range does not establish an exact first arrival of migrants or the duration of population change.

Parker Pearson's 2021 article provides the burial range. Vander Linden's 2024 synthesis supplies the broader early-phase chronology and regional context. Their accounts draw on overlapping archaeological literature; they are not independent sets of radiocarbon measurements.

Independent source and final reviews approved the unchanged candidate, all five display fields and the complete selection note. The final check verified thirty-two frozen inputs and six text hashes. Both review processes exited successfully. The underlying dating chapter cited by Parker Pearson was not independently read, and neither review claims direct examination of archaeological material.

The dataset remains `awaiting-runtime-support`. Publication as research does not make the event available in gameplay.
