# AD 100–199: public rules and canonical texts

The [dataset](ad0100-supplement.json) contains two independently approved events, four sources and ten English display fields.

Two English research records concern Palmyra's publication of revised municipal tax rules in 137 and preparation of the Xiping Stone Classics in Luoyang circa 175–183. The tariff record concerns local taxation and an effort to reduce disputes, without claiming that the effort succeeded. Its older provisions are not all assigned to 137.

The Xiping interval preserves the museum's supplied start and completion years and Tian Mi's approximate dating. It concerns a state-sponsored stone edition, not the original composition of the Confucian classics or later restoration of the monuments.

Evidence for Palmyra comes from a public scholarly extract and abstract; the complete article and chapter were not inspected. The Xiping evidence comes from public museum and university research accounts. No original inscription or monument was inspected, and no independent calendar conversion was performed.

Independent source and final reviews approved all ten display fields and both selection notes without historical corrections. A separate metadata review then approved exactly five changes: four reference-use statements adopt the brief's standard wording, and the Palace Museum source retains its official Chinese title. All event fields remain unchanged. Each review process exited successfully, and the original reports remain preserved.

These are separate English research records with awaiting-runtime-support status. Publication does not activate them in gameplay or establish comprehensive coverage of the century.
