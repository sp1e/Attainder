## Evidence and chronology

Each event cites one Key Events entry of the Metropolitan Museum of Art's Heilbrunn Timeline of Art History, from the pages "Mexico, 1000 B.C.–1 A.D.", "Korea, 1000 B.C.–1 A.D.", "Central and Southern Andes, 1000 B.C.–1 A.D.", "Mesopotamia, 1000 B.C.–1 A.D.", "Western North Africa, 1000 B.C.–1 A.D.". Each source record names the entry by its date phrase and opening words. The research session read the page text only; no images, object records or primary editions were examined. The Met dates most entries with "ca.", and the records keep that as approximate dates or ranges, with `noteEn` where the entry leaves the year of a single act open. Phoenician colonists found Carthage, by tradition: `historicalStatus` is `uncertain` because the entry gives the date as tradition or the cause as a possibility.

## Review and withholding

A fresh Claude subagent received only the delivered files, opened every cited page and checked each field against its entry. It corrected two selection notes: one named a Gulf Coast heartland and one a Bronze Age period that the cited entries do not give. All corrections are applied. Every page loaded; the site redirects each URL to the same page under `/pt/toah/ht/`.

Chavín de Huántar's pilgrimage phase (about 850 BCE) was withheld because the catalog already holds a Chavín ceremonial-centre record (1200 BCE) for the same site. Duplicate checks ran against the runtime catalog and every English dataset in this folder, by id and by historical episode.

Ruling: one institutional timeline supports each record, and each summary stays within its entry. This supplement does not establish complete regional or annual coverage. Compatible English runtime support remains pending; the gameplay catalog, legacy locales and coverage counts remain unchanged.
