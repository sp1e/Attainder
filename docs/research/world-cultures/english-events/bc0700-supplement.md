# Jerwan's stone aqueduct: English research supplement

The [dataset](bc0700-supplement.json) contains one approved event, two sources and five English display fields, at importance 1.

This supplement records the construction of the Jerwan aqueduct around 690 BCE, using two sources. Columbia's monument profile supplies that date. Stampoultzidis and colleagues place Jerwan in the fourth stage of the wider Nineveh canal programme, which they date to 703–688 BCE; their 688 date for the Bavian inscription does not independently establish the aqueduct's completion year.

The record concerns the stone crossing and its role in Nineveh's water supply. It makes no invention-priority, Hanging Gardens or quantified agricultural-benefit claim. The wider programme's chronology is retained as context, without clipping its beginning into this research window. Coverage remains partial.
