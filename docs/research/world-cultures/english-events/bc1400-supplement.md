## Evidence and chronology

Each event cites one Key Events entry of the Metropolitan Museum of Art's Heilbrunn Timeline of Art History, from the pages "Anatolia and the Caucasus, 2000–1000 B.C.", "Southern Europe, 2000–1000 B.C.", "Mesoamerica and Central America, 2000–1000 B.C.", "Iran, 2000–1000 B.C.". Each source record names the entry by its date phrase and opening words. The research session read the page text only; no images, object records or primary editions were examined. The Met dates most entries with "ca.", and the records keep that as approximate dates or ranges, with `noteEn` where the entry leaves the year of a single act open.

## Review and withholding

A fresh Claude subagent received only the delivered files, opened every cited page and checked each field against its entry. It confirmed all four records without correction. All corrections are applied. Every page loaded; the site redirects each URL to the same page under `/pt/toah/ht/`.

The remodelling of the San Lorenzo plateau (about 1400 BCE) was withheld because the catalog's San Lorenzo record (1500–900 BCE) covers the site. Soconusco burial goods, Tlatilco figurines, the Paso de la Amada celt and the Kassite boundary stones (1350–1000 BCE) were left for a later pass. Duplicate checks ran against the runtime catalog and every English dataset in this folder, by id and by historical episode.

Ruling: one institutional timeline supports each record, and each summary stays within its entry. This supplement does not establish complete regional or annual coverage. Compatible English runtime support remains pending; the gameplay catalog, legacy locales and coverage counts remain unchanged.
