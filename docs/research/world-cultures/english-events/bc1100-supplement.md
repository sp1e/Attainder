The Ritidian entry follows Mike T. Carson's archaeological phase of about 1100–700 BCE. It describes more intensive coastal settlement activity and differences between material remains outside cave entrances and at the beach-ridge settlement. The full phase is retained. The record does not date the first settlement of Guam, a particular painting or the manufacture of bone spear points to 1100 BCE. The source's earlier occupation and later inland developments remain distinct.

The Wu Geng entry follows Jordan Thomas Christopher's 1042–1039 BCE chronology for the rebellion and its suppression. It is distinct from Zhou's earlier conquest of Shang. The dates remain attributed rather than presented as a reconciliation of competing early Zhou chronologies. The account's later territorial grants and settlement policies are not all assigned to the rebellion's interval. The publisher labels the issue September 2025 and the online publication 6 April 2026; source metadata preserves both.
