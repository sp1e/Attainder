## Evidence and chronology

The La Bastida entry concerns the walls and towers defending an Argaric centre in southeastern Spain. The [2014 article's public abstract](https://portalrecerca.uab.cat/en/publications/the-la-bastida-fortification-new-light-and-new-questions-on-early/) dates the fortification to 2200–2100 cal BC. A [later fieldwork abstract](https://portalrecerca.uab.cat/ca/publications/archaeological-excavation-at-la-bastida-2018-april-june-fieldwork/) provisionally associates the original construction with the wider earliest settlement phase, about 2200–2025 BC. Both qualifications remain visible; neither establishes an exact building year or a particular siege.

The Utuhegal entry uses the explicit approximate date of 2120 BC in [Sazonov, Espak and Johandi's history](https://www.etag.ee/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/Espak-L%C3%A4his-Ida-ajalugu.pdf), printed page 141. [Gadd's public Cambridge summary](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/cambridge-ancient-history/babylonia-c-21201800-bc/85EDA95754C326B21CB3261F95466CFB) supports the victory's political consequence: a brief restoration of Sumerian kingship to Uruk. The chapter title does not supply an independent battle date.
