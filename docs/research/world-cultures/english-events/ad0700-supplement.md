# AD 700–799: English research supplement

The [dataset](ad0700-supplement.json) contains two independently approved events, four sources and ten English display fields. Both events are dated AD 732 and have importance 1. They concern royal commemoration in Central Asia and religious patronage in Central Java.

## Evidence and chronology

The Kül Tigin record concerns Bilge Kagan's erection of a memorial in 732, distinct from Kül Tigin's death in 731. Marmara University's departmental booklet and Bostan's scholarly book-review abstract support the date, royal commission, Turkic and Chinese texts, and political content. The event makes no claim to establish a state or invent a writing system.

The Canggal record concerns Sanjaya's linga dedication. Rusyanti and colleagues describe the dated inscription and its dedication for the people's safety and welfare. Klokke identifies Canggal as Central Java's first dated inscription and discusses competing reconstructions of the region's dynasties. The event adopts no kingdom-foundation date, dynastic model or claim of population-wide conversion.

All four consulted sources are modern institutional or scholarly accounts. No primary inscription edition or PDF image was examined. After a technical timeout, an ordinary download from the same BRIN URL supplied the required passage. These access limits remain in the source records.

## Review and withholding

Independent source and English review approved both events and all ten display fields without correction. A separate metadata review held one selection note because its catalog-comparison sentence fell outside that review's evidence. The exact deletion was applied in a separate candidate; a fresh correction review approved the result. All three processes exited successfully. The historical fields, sources, dates and English display text remained unchanged, and the original HOLD report is preserved.

The records are distinct from the catalog's later Kalasan and Sewu inscriptions and Uyghur state formation. Gopala's approximate reign did not establish a separate dated foundation event and was excluded. Earlier held regional leads remain unresolved.

Ruling: retain the dated acts of commemoration and dedication at importance 1, with their limited documentary significance. This focused supplement does not establish complete annual or regional coverage.
