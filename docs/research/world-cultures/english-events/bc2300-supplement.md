# Sannai Maruyama: the end of a Jomon settlement

The [dataset](bc2300-supplement.json) contains one independently approved event, two sources and five English display fields.

This English research supplement records the archaeological occupation endpoint of Sannai Maruyama in northern Japan. Habu's chronology ends around 2300 cal BC; the official heritage summary extends the sizeable settlement to 2200 BCE. Both alternatives remain explicit and uncertain. The numeric span does not represent a century-long departure or simultaneous evacuation of every house.

Earlier changes in subsistence, settlement size and ritual have their own chronology. The record supplies no proven single cause, destination or exact departure year. It is a notable settlement-history event, with importance 1.

The original independent review placed the three-source draft on HOLD after one PDF could not be read. That source was removed, and the text was narrowed to the two sources the reviewer read. The original HOLD remains part of the review history. Evidence for the retained record consists of Habu's extracted PDF text and the official heritage account; no original archaeological material or PDF figures were visually examined. Source metadata uses the standard reference-only statement, with original English paraphrases.

A first final review caught a remaining reference to the removed source in one chronology field. A fresh correction review approved removal of that clause and verified that all six event text fields, including the selection note, stayed unchanged. It also verified the original author's complete input against the neutral source-review input; the latter omitted only the selection note. Both original HOLD reports remain preserved, and all three review processes exited successfully.
