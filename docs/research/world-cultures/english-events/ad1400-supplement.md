# AD 1400–1499: English research supplement

The [dataset](ad1400-supplement.json) contains two independently approved events, two sources and ten English display fields. They concern religious institutions in Ethiopia and Pegu. Both have importance 2.

## Evidence and chronology

Pinggera's chapter dates the council of Dabra Metmaq to 1450 and describes its decision in favor of Sabbath observance alongside Sunday. The event records that decision without dating the origin of the practice or claiming immediate universal compliance. The passage was read as extracted PDF text; parent-volume metadata was not established.

Leider's study dates Dhammazedi's Sri Lankan mission to 1475 and Pegu's new Kalyani ordination ground to 1476. The event joins these linked milestones, preserving both years. It assigns no continuous voyage duration, complete reordination schedule or inscription-production date. Nearby doubts about separate Arakanese missions supply no support for those missions here.

The original selection notes had been intentionally omitted from the neutral source-review packet; the final check read and approved them without replacement. Both review processes exited successfully.
