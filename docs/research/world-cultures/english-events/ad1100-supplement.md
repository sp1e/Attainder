# AD 1100–1199: English research supplement

The [dataset](ad1100-supplement.json) contains two independently approved events, four sources and ten English display fields. They concern al-Qarawiyyin's mosque expansion in Fez and Nur al-Din's hospital in Damascus. Both have importance 1.

## Evidence and chronology

Archnet places Ali ibn Yusuf's mosque expansion around 1134–1143. Serrat and colleagues give completion in 1143–44 and separately discuss a structural inscription dated 1136. The record preserves the full 1134–1144 span and distinguishes the earlier structural date from completion of the campaign. It makes no university-foundation claim. The archaeological article was read through extracted PDF text; its images were not inspected.

Museum With No Frontiers dates the Damascus hospital's main building to AD 1154 using a described foundation inscription. Archnet supplies the same AD year and separates the later extension. Their Hijri labels differ, 549 AH and 548 AH respectively; the date note preserves that difference without making a new conversion. Descriptions of patient care and medical teaching concern the institution and do not date every activity to its building year.

## Review and withholding

Fresh independent source and English review approved both events, all four sources and all ten display fields without changes. A separate final check approved both selection notes, the unchanged data and schema choices. Both processes exited successfully, and the original candidate remained unchanged before publication metadata was added.

Hantang remained outside the delivery after the museum-catalogue PDF could not be read because of connection timeouts. Mayapan was excluded because it overlapped existing political-phase material and supplied no distinct newly dated act. Previously held regional leads remain unresolved.

Ruling: retain the specific building campaigns with their chronological limits and modest significance. This focused supplement does not establish complete annual or regional coverage. Compatible English runtime support remains pending; the gameplay catalog, legacy locales and coverage counts remain unchanged.
