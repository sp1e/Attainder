# 2600–2501 BCE: English research supplement

The [dataset](bc2600-supplement.json) contains 2 independently approved events and 2 source records, 1 at importance 2, 1 at importance 1. The catalog marked this window unresearched.

## Evidence and chronology

Each event cites one Key Events entry of the Metropolitan Museum of Art's Heilbrunn Timeline of Art History, from the pages "South Asia, 8000–2000 B.C.", "Iran, 8000–2000 B.C.". Each source record names the entry by its date phrase and opening words. The research session read the page text only; no images, object records or primary editions were examined. The Met dates most entries with "ca.", and the records keep that as approximate dates or ranges, with `noteEn` where the entry leaves the year of a single act open.

## Review and withholding

A fresh Claude subagent received only the delivered files, opened every cited page and checked each field against its entry. It corrected both records: the Indus title and geography named the Indus Valley, which the cited entry does not, and the chlorite record said the vessels were carved in workshops and called them luxury goods. All corrections are applied. Every page loaded; the site redirects each URL to the same page under `/pt/toah/ht/`.

The Met's Egypt page dates the Old Kingdom from about 2649 BCE, before this window, and gives no separate date for the Giza pyramids, so no Egyptian record was added. No other Key Events entry on the pages read starts in this window. Duplicate checks ran against the runtime catalog and every English dataset in this folder, by id and by historical episode.

Ruling: one institutional timeline supports each record, and each summary stays within its entry. This supplement does not establish complete regional or annual coverage. Compatible English runtime support remains pending; the gameplay catalog, legacy locales and coverage counts remain unchanged.
