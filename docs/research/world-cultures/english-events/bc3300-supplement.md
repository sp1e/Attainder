# English research supplement: 3300–3201 BC

The [dataset](bc3300-supplement.json) contains two independently approved events, three sources and ten English display fields. It examines Fanshan's elite burial assemblage and the initial expansion of the Yamnaya archaeological complex. The complete approximate ranges, 3300–2900 BC and 3300–3000 BC, extend beyond the research window. They describe a cemetery assemblage and a broad historical process, respectively; neither is a single-year event.

The sources are academic syntheses, including a Cambridge doctoral thesis, a Cambridge Elements monograph and an independently authored Uppsala monograph. Their citations do not imply that every underlying excavation report or genetic study was independently accessed. The research does not infer named rulers at Fanshan, a unified Yamnaya state, a uniform language, the invention of riding, or a particular migration mechanism.

Existing Liangzhu entries concern separate dam works and the principal city-construction episode around 3000–2850 BC. Fanshan's earlier mortuary assemblage is recorded separately. The Yamnaya expansion is distinct from the catalog's more narrowly scoped cattle-specialization record around 3100 BC.

Several leads remain unused. Jebel Aruda's primary excavation report provides a broad radiocarbon estimate and no secure settlement duration. Previously withheld Uruk-sealing, writing-origin and palace episodes remain withheld. Two Nature routes returned authentication redirects, and a Smithsonian page rejected access; these routes and alternative copies were not pursued.

Independent source and English review approved all three sources and all ten display fields. It required one exact correction: lowering Fanshan's importance from 2 to 1, because the cemetery's significance does not by itself establish a major regional transformation. Yamnaya's continental expansion retains importance 3. A fresh final review confirmed the correction and every unchanged field; both processes exited successfully. The original HOLD report remains preserved.

This is a bounded addition, not complete coverage of the century or all world regions. It is English-only research awaiting runtime support. Runtime event counts, gameplay availability and coverage statuses are unchanged. Research approval is separate from compatible loading and in-game verification.
