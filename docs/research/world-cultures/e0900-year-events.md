# Historical events starting in AD 900–999

This batch adds 44 approved events and 43 source records. Together with the preceding century, the catalog contains 88 events and 82 source records. Both centuries retain partial coverage. All 44 earlier events and their 39 source records remain unchanged.

The new batch contains 29 exact-year dates, six approximate dates, five ranges and four disputed spans. Tula retains its full approximate 900–1150 phase as one record. Greenland, Cluny, Madinat al-Zahra and Mieszko retain competing dates; no unrecorded calendar conversion was introduced.

## Source review and corrections

Two fresh source-only Codex sessions assessed the 45 initial event records and all 41 cited source records. Each event was one composite claim, including its complete date, title, summary, geography and selection rationale. The reviewers opened the cited passages themselves, including rendered scans, without the authors' drafting reports.

All ten follow-up source records were inspected. The final set consists of 39 initially confirmed events and five follow-up confirmations.

- Cluny: the competing years concern the monastery's foundation. The initial text incorrectly assigned both years to its charter. The revised summary and rationale preserve the 909/910 disagreement.
- Kyiv: the geography now names the city. Attaching present-day Ukraine to the whole Rus realm overstated the geographic correspondence.
- Chetro Ketl: the revised summary distinguishes the older tree-ring compilation from the 1978 nomination containing it. The follow-up also corrected the source locator to W. James Judge's appended 1976 paper, printed page 2, PDF page 25. It was mislabelled as a Section 8 continuation sheet. The reviewer rendered and inspected the intended passage; no event date changed.
- Al-Mansuriya: the replacement uses Sakly's source-supplied 945–948 foundation bounds and preserves his narrower 947/948 wording and the Met's 947. The ID changes from `ev-0947-fatimiderna-grundar-al-mansuriya` to `ev-0945-fatimiderna-grundar-al-mansuriya`; only the replacement remains. The interval describes uncertain timing, with no assertion of continuous construction over those years.
- Schroda: a new approximate settlement event preserves the distinction between initial settlement, later occupation phases, trade evidence and uncertain abandonment. It does not date every artefact to the settlement year. Four reopened scholarly or institutional sources support the qualified account.

Two initial candidates are withheld. The Kokinshū source gives completion by about 905, which supplies an upper bound rather than a best-estimate event year. Shanga's initial source supported a qualified Building H account after a geographic correction, but additional opened archaeological literature left phase identification and competing mosque interpretations unresolved. The initial supported core did not settle those later concerns. Neither candidate received an invented date to fill the gap.

The retained source set comprises 38 initial records and five added records. One initial record has the NPS locator correction described above. Three unused original records were removed. The existing Met Fatimid record already covers the replacement's museum claims, so the replacement reuses it; the narrower redundant recovery record was omitted. This preserves the original scoped evidence without claiming the two metadata records were identical.

## Coverage and rulings

Each event is counted once in its research region. These categories do not define ancient political borders.

Ruling: retain partial coverage. One southern African event and no Oceanian event cannot establish a worldwide annual survey. Empty years remain research gaps.

Ruling: withhold Shanga and Kokinshū while retaining their initial audit outcomes. A supported account can still require reassessment when additional chronology problems emerge. The cost is two gaps rather than unsupported annual precision.

Ruling: keep the wider source-supplied Mansuriya window and all narrower datings visible. Keep the conflicting Schroda phase and abandonment accounts separate from its approximate settlement date. Treating either range as a precise construction schedule would misrepresent the sources.

Ruling: preserve genuine long phases once. Tula's later endpoint does not make AD 1000–1150 researched, and the earlier Ife and Igbo-Ukwu records were not duplicated. A dated Maya inscription remains an inscription date, without an inferred urban-abandonment year.
