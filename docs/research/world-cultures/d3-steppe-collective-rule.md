## Uyghur Khaganate

Huaixin qaghan is the source designation for the ruler whose reign begins in 795. Mackerras prefers death in 808 and explains the competing 805 endpoint; both reported intervals contain the 800 bookmark. Identity and office in 800 are sourced, while the disputed reign chronology remains uncertain. Birth and daily residence are unknown. Claims: u-holder and u-succession. [Mackerras, printed p. 94 and note 4](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/download/article-file/3797515).

Thierry’s public abstract identifies Boquq with Huaixin through a numismatic argument. It remains an attributed interpretation. Yoshida distinguishes the seventh ruler’s 795–808 chronology from the eighth ruler praised in the stele’s opening dedication. Similar titles do not supply an unrestricted alias list. Claims: u-names and u-agency. [Thierry, public abstract only](https://poj.peeters-leuven.be/content.php?id=2004301&journal_code=TURC&url=article), [Yoshida, opening identification and seventh-qaghan discussion](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/karabalgasun-the-inscription/).

The dossier describes a khagan-headed empire with tribal components and differentiated offices. The 742 coalition does not establish an unchanged council of equal sovereign co-rulers in 800. Later stele offices and activities dated 807 or 821 do not fill an 800 roster. Princess Xian’an’s successive-katun narrative supplies qualified marriage context; a complete named bookmark household remains withheld. Claims: u-form, u-marriage, u-agency and u-engine. [Cambridge public Summary](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/cambridge-history-of-early-inner-asia/uighurs/5EDCA8414EB81B737294DA7F21575D72), [Mackerras, printed pp. 98–101](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/download/article-file/3797515).

## Icelandic Commonwealth

Council decisions, courts, the elected lawspeaker and local enforcement require distinct roles. The lawspeaker’s three-year term and limited legal powers do not supply a monarch with general executive command. Byock also describes increasing concentration among large chieftains in the late twelfth and thirteenth centuries. Earlier arrangements are kept within their chronology. Claims: i-form, i-lawspeaker, i-kin, i-agency and i-succession. [Alþingi, printed pp. 6–7](https://www.althingi.is/pdf/Althingi2017_enska.pdf), [Byock, printed pp. 3–6 and 12–14](https://www.viking.ucla.edu/publications/articles/icelandic_allthing.pdf).

No exact 1200 lawspeaker or council roster was established in the bounded corpus. This is a research gap. Snorrastofa records Snorri’s marriage to Herdís Bersadóttir in 1199 and his later lawspeaker term in 1215–1218. The marriage remains contextual family evidence, with no 1200 office or national royal household assigned. Claims: i-holder and i-marriage. [Snorrastofa, PDF page 7 timeline](https://www.snorrastofa.is/media/1/snorri-syningarskra-vefur-01.pdf).

## Caesar’s source account

Book VI.23 reports no common magistrate in peace, while district and canton chiefs administer justice. It also describes selected wartime commanders with coercive powers and expedition leaders recruiting followers in an assembly. Those conditions and local powers remain explicit. This is a Roman outsider’s account within a limited geographical field, with no universal assertion about all Germanic peoples. Claims: g-positive, g-scope and g-holder. [York-hosted translation, VI.21–23](https://www.yorku.ca/pswarney/Texts/bel-gal-6.htm), [Fordham, introduction and section 23](https://sourcebooks.fordham.edu/source/51caesar-germans.asp).

The publisher dates Book VI’s campaign events to 53 BC; Fordham heads the extract approximately 51 BCE. Neither establishes an observation day or a named community’s constitution on an exact bookmark. The exact date, community, holder and seat remain withheld. Claim: g-date. During review the UK publisher URL failed; the same publisher’s US page, with ISBN 9780862920883, supplied the actual description and metadata. Only that public material was read. [Bloomsbury US, Description and Product details](https://www.bloomsbury.com/us/caesar-gallic-war-vi-9780862920883/).
