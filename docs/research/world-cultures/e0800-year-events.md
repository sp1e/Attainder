# Historical events starting in AD 800–899

This batch supplies 44 approved events and 39 source records to the existing chronicle and turn-end catalog. It is the first populated century. Coverage is partial: the selection includes eight broad regions, with substantial gaps within them and no retained event for Oceania.

The catalog preserves 34 exact-year dates, six approximate dates, three ranges and one disputed span. Exact-year status supplies no month or day beyond the cited passage. Ife's approximate 800–1000 development and Igbo-Ukwu's disputed 800–999 dating remain single records. Their later endpoints do not establish researched coverage for the following century.

## Regional limits

Each event is counted once in its research region. These categories organize the audit; they establish no ancient political boundary.

Ruling: mark AD 800–899 partial. The researched selection leaves explicit regional and subject gaps. Promoting it to reviewed would overstate the work; an empty year does not establish that nothing happened.

Ruling: retain uncertain ranges once, including spans beyond 899. Narrowing them to fit the publication batch would invent precision, while copying them into the next century would duplicate the same historical development. Dongola's early-ninth-century wording remains visible alongside its conservative enclosing numeric century.

Ruling: keep original chronology and source-supplied calendar correspondences. The catalog performs no new conversions. Approximate archaeological phases, traditional accounts and competing dates keep their qualifications; a terminal monument date does not become an abandonment year.
