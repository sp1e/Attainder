# BC 1200–1101 research candidate

This candidate adds eight event records and ten cited sources, with 40 English event-field translations and 20 Swedish source-display translations. The records concern the palace destructions at Pylos and Thebes, Hallur's iron-bearing phase transition, pearl millet in the Gajiganna sequence, Chavín de Huántar, Ramesses III's encounter with the Sea Peoples, the Deir el-Medina work stoppage and the fall of the Kassite dynasty.

A fresh independent source-review process returned PASS for all eight events and all ten sources. The reconciliation changes exactly one source leaf: Hallur's locator expands from manuscript/PDF page 17 to pages 16–17. No event field or date changes. Its Swedish locator translation and source-text hash are updated; the other 59 bilingual entries remain unchanged from their language drafts. These are source-review findings and prepared data, not a claim that the batch is merged.

Chronological qualifications remain explicit. Pylos retains approximately 1200 BC alongside the alternative Early Late Helladic IIIC placement, 1190/1180–1150 BC. The Sea Peoples episode retains 1180, 1177 and 1176/1175 BC as alternatives for the eighth regnal year. Hallur keeps the complete modelled 1200–1100 BC transition interval. Gajiganna retains approximately 1200 calibrated BC. The two 1155 BC records explicitly attribute their dates to the respective museums. No event year is calculated from reign endpoints, and enclosing intervals do not describe continuous fighting or annual occurrence.

The source review checked accessible museum and scholarly interpretations, including Middleton's review rather than the underlying excavation chapters. The Hallur and Weninger–Jung PDFs were read as extracted text; no PDF page image was viewed. The strike evidence uses the public written podcast summary and papyrus catalogue description. No podcast audio, gated translation or original ancient document was read. Earlier author retrieval assertions remain separate from the reviewer's independently observed passages. The review's technical command-length failure concerned saving its trace, not source access; no source-access denial was bypassed.

All nine working regions were searched. East Asia, Southeast Asia, Oceania, the steppe and Central Asia have no newly retained record; the represented regions remain incomplete too. Fu Hao's alternative 1250/1200 BC dates, unresolved Teouma chronology, a gated copper-production study and other leads remain outside this candidate. Only 1200–1101 BC changes to partial coverage. Hallur's later endpoint does not establish researched coverage for 1100 BC, and empty years remain research gaps.
