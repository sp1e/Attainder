# AD 200–299: supplementary events

This supplement adds two events and three sources to the existing partial catalog. It supplies ten English event translations and six Swedish source-panel translations. The existing seventeen events and their qualifications remain unchanged.

## Evidence and dates

- Septimius Severus died at York on 4 February 211. [English Heritage](https://www.english-heritage.org.uk/visit/places/hadrians-wall/hadrians-wall-history-and-stories/septimius-severus/) supplies the date and the context of his illness and renewed northern campaign.
- Decius and his elder son Herennius died at Abritus in 251; the troops then proclaimed Trebonianus Gallus. [David Potter's public chapter summary](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/generalship-in-ancient-greece-rome-and-byzantium/decius-and-the-battle-near-abritus/A61EF5E3D0B792FE00E4F8D2A30BCECD) places the end of the fatal campaign in May 251. [Sabine Huebner's article](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/4DD870D473D3DDB1DF0328CE2C7E3280/S1047759421000349a.pdf/the-plague-of-cyprian-a-revised-view-of-the-origin-and-spread-of-a-3rd-c-ce-pandemic.pdf), printed pages 156–157, gives early June and discusses May/June. The record keeps this disagreement visible in both languages.

## Review and limits

A fresh, separate reviewer checked both events, all three sources and all sixteen language pairs against frozen candidate files. The review passed with no required replacements, and the review process exited successfully. The PDF was checked through extracted text; no visual page inspection is claimed. The Potter source supports only the publicly available summary used here.

Ruling: retain the exact year 251 while showing the competing months. Choosing one month without qualification would conceal disagreement in the consulted sources.

The supplement covers European events. It does not close the other regional gaps, approve the original withheld candidates or mark any additional year as researched.
